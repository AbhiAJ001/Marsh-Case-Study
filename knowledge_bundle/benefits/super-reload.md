# Super Reload

Unlimited refills of Sum Insured if the cover is exhausted during the policy year.

## How It Works

If the Sum Insured is exhausted during a policy year, Super Reload automatically replenishes the full Sum Insured amount — unlimited times. There is no cap on the number of reloads within a single policy year.
