# Claim Protect

100% of out-of-pocket expenses covered during hospitalisation claims.

## How It Works

When a policyholder files a claim, Claim Protect ensures that 100% of out-of-pocket (OOP) expenses are covered. This means the insured pays nothing beyond the premium — no co-payments, no deductibles eating into the claim amount.
